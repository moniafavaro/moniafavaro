<h2 align="center">Featured Projects</h2>

| Project | What I explored | Tools |
|---|---|---|
| [**Anime & Manga Data Analysis**](https://github.com/moniafavaro/anime-and-manga-data-analysis) | Cleaned and analysed about 30,000 anime and 83,000 manga titles from MyAnimeList to see how genres, ratings and popularity changed over time.<br><br>**Finding:** median TV series length fell from 52 episodes in the 1960s to 12 in the 2020s. | Python, pandas, seaborn, SciPy |
| [**Titanic Survival Analysis**](https://github.com/moniafavaro/Titanic-Survival-Analysis) | Explored what influenced survival, then trained two models to predict it.<br><br>**Finding:** sex was the strongest predictor (73% of women survived vs 19% of men), and logistic regression (84% test accuracy) beat random forest (79%). | Python, pandas, scikit-learn |
| [**Goodreads Reading Trends**](https://github.com/moniafavaro/Goodreads-Reading-Trends) | Built an ETL pipeline that cleans, validates and loads book data into PostgreSQL, then analysed it, modelled ratings and built a book recommender.<br><br>**Finding:** page count and popularity barely explain a book's rating. | Python, PostgreSQL, SQLAlchemy, scikit-learn |
